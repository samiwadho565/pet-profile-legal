Delete your Pet Profile account

Pet Profile (by Pet Panda)
Option 1 — in the app

Open Pet Profile → Profile → Delete Account → type DELETE ACCOUNT → confirm. Your account and all your data are deleted immediately and permanently.

Option 2 — without the app

Email thepetsprofile@gmail.com from the email address you registered with, with the subject "Delete my account". We will delete your account within 7 days and confirm by email.

What gets deleted

Your profile (name, email), pets, medical records and documents, diet plans, reminders, Mating Board posts and bids, Lost & Found posts, all uploaded photos, push notification tokens and your block list.

What may be kept

Reports you filed about other users' content may be kept in anonymised form for safety purposes. Crash reports in Firebase Crashlytics are deleted automatically after 90 days.
