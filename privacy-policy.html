Pet Profile — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

1. About Pet Profile

Pet Profile is a pet management application developed and operated by Pet Profile. By using this app, you agree to this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following information to provide our services: • Account Information: Your name and email address when you register. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and profile picture from your Google account. • Pet Information: Name, species, breed, age, weight, gender, photo, and health notes for each pet you add. • Medical Records: Vaccinations, vet visits, medications, dosage details, and any medical documents (e.g. prescriptions, lab reports) you upload. • Diet Plans: Feeding schedules, food types, portion sizes, and allergy information. • Mating Board Posts & Bids: When you post a mating request or place a bid, we collect your pet details (including any health information you choose to share, such as vaccination status), photos, your contact number, location (city), any message or stud fee, and any description you provide. Mating posts are visible to all users, but your phone number is private: a post owner's number is shared only with the bidder whose bid they accept, and a bid (including the bidder's number) is visible only to the owner of that post. • Lost & Found Posts: When you post a lost or found pet, we collect pet details and photos, your contact name and phone number, the last-seen location (address and, if you pick it on the map, its map coordinates), city, and any reward you mention. This information is publicly visible to other users so they can contact you. • PetDoc AI (chat and Scan): Messages and pet photos you send to the PetDoc AI assistant, including photos taken with PetDoc Scan, are sent through our secure server to Anthropic's Claude API to generate a response. If Claude is unavailable, the Google Gemini API is used as a backup. We do not store your chat messages or chat photos; we only keep a count of how many PetDoc messages and scans each account uses, to apply plan limits and prevent abuse. • Saved Health Scans: If you tap "Save to pet profile" after a PetDoc Scan, we store a small copy of the scan photo and the scan result (health score, likely breed, findings and tips) in that pet's health history. Saved scans are visible only to you in the app and you can delete them at any time. Scans you do not save are not stored. • Alarm Times: The times of your reminder, feeding and medicine alarms are saved with your account, so your alarms can be set again when you sign in on another phone or reinstall the app. • Reports & Blocks: If you report content or block a user, we store the report (reason, optional details and a copy of the reported content) and your block list. • Device & Diagnostic Data: A push notification token (Firebase Cloud Messaging) so we can send you bid and app notifications, and crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics) that include device model, operating system version and app state at the time of a crash. • Advertising Data: Pet Profile shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location (based on IP), device and app information, and how you interact with ads. Users in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown, and can change their choice at any time in Profile → Ad privacy settings. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or turn off ad personalisation, in your phone's settings. • Purchases & Subscriptions: If you buy Pet Profile Premium, payment is handled entirely by Google Play (or the Apple App Store on iOS). We never receive or store your card or payment details. We use RevenueCat to manage subscriptions: RevenueCat and our server receive your account ID (a random user ID), the product you bought, purchase and renewal dates, free-trial status, the store transaction ID and whether your subscription is active, cancelled or refunded. We use this only to unlock Premium features, restore your purchase on another device and keep your plan up to date. • Device Permissions: Camera and photo access (via the system photo picker) only when you choose to add a photo. Notification and exact-alarm permission for medicine and feeding reminders. Pet Profile does not access your device's GPS location.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to: • Operate and improve the Pet Profile app. • Sync your pet data securely across your devices. • Display your Mating Board and Lost & Found posts to other users. • Send alarm notifications for medicine and feeding reminders you configure, and push notifications about your posts and bids. • Generate pet health summaries (PDF) on your device, which you can choose to share. • Provide AI-powered pet care guidance via PetDoc. • Review reported content and keep the community safe. • Diagnose crashes and fix bugs. • Process your Premium subscription, unlock Premium features (such as unlimited pets, weight charts, PetDoc photos and an ad-free experience) and apply Free plan limits. We do not sell or rent your personal information. Pet Profile shows ads served by Google AdMob (banner and full-screen ads) — see "Advertising Data" above. Premium subscribers do not see ads. We never share your pet details, medical records, PetDoc chats or contact numbers with advertisers.

5. PetDoc AI Assistant

PetDoc is an AI-powered assistant that answers pet care questions and can look at photos of your pet. Please note: • Your messages and photos are processed by Anthropic's Claude API, with the Google Gemini API as a backup. By using PetDoc, you also agree to the applicable terms of these providers. Under Anthropic's commercial terms, content sent through its API is not used to train its models. Google may use content submitted through the Gemini API to improve its products. • PetDoc Scan looks at a photo of your pet and gives a health score, likely breed and things to watch. It is a visual check only — it cannot see everything a vet can and may be wrong. • PetDoc provides general information only. It is not a licensed veterinarian and its responses should not be treated as professional veterinary advice, diagnosis, or treatment. In an emergency, contact a veterinarian immediately. • Do not share sensitive personal information (e.g., your full name, address, financial information) or photos of people in the AI chat. • You can report any PetDoc response you think is wrong, harmful or inappropriate using the "Report response" link under it. • PetDoc usage may be limited to a number of messages and scans per day or per week. Limits and photo analysis depend on your plan (Free or Premium). • Pet Profile and Pet Panda are not liable for any decisions made based on PetDoc's responses.

6. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored securely using Supabase, a trusted cloud platform. All data is encrypted in transit via HTTPS/TLS. Photos and documents are stored in Supabase Storage. We implement industry-standard security measures. However, no internet transmission is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security of your data.

7. Data Sharing

We do not share your personal data except: • Service Providers that process data on our behalf: Supabase (database, authentication and storage), Anthropic (Claude API, AI responses; see https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy), Google Gemini API (backup AI responses), Google Firebase (push notifications via Firebase Cloud Messaging and crash reports via Firebase Crashlytics), Google Sign-In (if you choose to sign in with Google), Google AdMob (ads), Google Play Billing (payments for Premium) and RevenueCat (subscription management; see https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy). Google processes advertising data as described in its Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy) and at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. • Other users: Mating Board and Lost & Found posts are visible to all app users. The contact number on a Lost & Found post is public so people can reach you. Mating Board phone numbers are shared only between a post owner and the bidder they accept, and a bidder's number is visible to the owner of the post they bid on. • Legal Requirements: If required by applicable law or to protect user safety.

8. Photos & Media

Pet photos and medical documents you upload are stored in our cloud storage and are shown inside the app only to you. They are stored at unguessable web addresses, so anyone who has the exact link could open the file — do not share these links if you want to keep them private. Photos of health scans you save to a pet's profile are stored the same way and are shown only to you. Mating post photos and Lost & Found photos are visible to all users as part of the post. Camera and photo access are used only when you actively choose to add or change a photo.

9. Notifications & Alarms

Pet Profile uses device notifications and alarms for medicine and feeding reminders. Alarm notifications are designed to alert you even when your phone is locked. You can disable notifications at any time from your device settings. Pet Profile can also remind you of a medical record's next due date (for example a vaccination) with a notification the day before and on the day. When you sign out, the alarms and reminders on that device are turned off; they are set again when you sign in. We are not responsible for missed alarms due to device settings, battery optimization, or operating system restrictions.

10. Your Rights & Account Deletion

You have the right to: • Access and correct your personal data within the app. • Remove your Mating Board posts, bids and Lost & Found posts at any time from within the app. • Opt out of notifications via your device settings. • Change your ad consent (European Economic Area, UK and Switzerland) in Profile → Ad privacy settings, and reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your device settings. • Delete your account and all associated data. How to delete your account: — In the app: Profile → Delete Account. Your account and data are deleted immediately and permanently. — Without the app: email thepetsprofile@gmail.com from your registered email address with the subject "Delete my account". We will delete your account within 7 days and confirm by email. When your account is deleted, we permanently delete your profile, pets, medical records, diet plans, reminders, Mating Board posts and bids, Lost & Found posts, uploaded photos and documents, push tokens, and block list. Reports you filed may be kept in anonymised form for safety purposes. Crash reports held by Firebase Crashlytics are deleted automatically by Google after 90 days.

11. Data Retention

We keep your data for as long as your account is active. Resolved or deleted posts are removed from our database when you delete them. Reported content and moderation records are kept only as long as needed to review the report and keep the community safe. All personal data is deleted when your account is deleted, as described above.

12. Children's Privacy

Pet Profile is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, please contact us and we will delete the account promptly.

Your use of the app and its community features is entirely at your own risk.
